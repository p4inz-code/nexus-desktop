# Privacy Policy

Nexus is built around a simple principle: your data should never leave your
device. This document explains exactly what is, and isn't, collected — both
by the application itself and by the manual processes around it (like
issuing you a beta key).

## The application

- **Zero network calls.** Nexus does not connect to the internet for any
  reason. Encryption, decryption, and license-key verification all happen
  locally on your machine.
- **Zero telemetry.** No analytics, no crash reporting, no usage tracking,
  no "anonymous" diagnostics sent anywhere.
- **No account.** There is nothing to sign up for and no server that knows
  you exist.
- **Your files stay on your device.** Nexus has no cloud sync or backup
  feature. Whatever you put in your vault stays in your vault, encrypted,
  on your machine, until you export it yourself.

Local log files (for troubleshooting crashes) stay on your device and are
never transmitted anywhere. See [Troubleshooting](docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md)
for where they live if you ever need to share one with us manually.

## The beta program (outside the app)

Because there's no automated purchase or account system, getting a license
key currently requires reaching out to us directly on Discord or by email.
That process does involve us seeing:

- Your Discord username, or the email address you contact us from.
- Whatever you tell us in that conversation (e.g., reporting a bug).

We use this only to issue your license key and provide support. We don't
sell it, share it with third parties, or use it for anything beyond
supporting the beta. If you'd rather not have any contact history with us
at all, the trade-off is that we currently have no way to issue a key
without one.

## What we don't do

- We don't track you across sessions, devices, or the web.
- We don't build a profile of you or your usage.
- We don't share any information with advertisers — there are no ads in
  Nexus, and there will not be.

## Changes to this policy

If this changes materially in the future (for example, if a paid version
introduces a payment processor), we'll update this document and announce it
via [Discord](https://discord.gg/8UKt8s5FbW) before the change takes effect.
