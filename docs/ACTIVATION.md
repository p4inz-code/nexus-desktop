# Activation & License Keys

## How keys work during the beta

Nexus is free during the public beta, but there's no automated purchase or
self-serve signup — keys are issued manually by Northbyte Studios. This is
a deliberate trade-off: no payment processor, no account system, no server
at all. The cost is that getting a key takes a human on the other end.

To request one:

- Discord: [discord.gg/8UKt8s5FbW](https://discord.gg/8UKt8s5FbW)
- Email: atharva.patil.cg@gmail.com

The first 20–30 active beta testers may, at Northbyte Studios' discretion,
receive a complimentary lifetime license as a thank-you. This isn't a
formal program with published eligibility rules — just tell us you're
using Nexus and we'll take it from there.

## Activating

When you first run Nexus, you'll be prompted for a license key. Enter it
exactly as given to you and follow the on-screen steps to set your master
password and generate your recovery phrase.

Keys are:

- **Non-transferable** — issued to you personally.
- **Bound to your machine.** Verification happens entirely offline, on your
  device — there's no server checking in.

## Moving to a new machine / reinstalling

Because activation is machine-bound and verified locally, there's currently
no self-service way to move a key to a new machine from inside the app. If
you need to reinstall Nexus or move to a different computer, reach out to
us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/8UKt8s5FbW) or by email and we'll help
you sort it out.

## If your key stops working

First check [Troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md) for common activation
issues. If that doesn't resolve it, contact us with your key and a
description of what happened — we'll look into it.

## About the beta-era verification model

Offline key verification during the free beta is intentionally lightweight
— proportionate to a free product with nothing to steal, not designed to
resist a determined attacker. We're upfront about this in
[SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). Full server-backed verification is planned
for the paid version (see [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md)).
