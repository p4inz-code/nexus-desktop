Privacy

What we see, and what we do not

Short version. The full policy is on GitHub.

The app

  • Your files stay on your PC. There is no cloud sync or backup service.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting and no usage tracking.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • One optional network call: the update check, to GitHub only. It sends nothing about you or your vault.

Getting a beta key

Because keys are issued by hand, we see your Discord name or the email you write from, and whatever you tell us. We use it only to issue your key and give support. We do not sell or share it.

This website

No cookies, no trackers and no requests to other sites. Pages are static files on GitHub Pages. GitHub, which hosts them, keeps its own server logs under its own policy.
