Questions

Common ones

How do I get a license key?

Ask on Discord or by email. Keys are issued by hand during the beta, so it can take a little while.

I forgot my master password.

Use your recovery phrase from the lock screen. If you also lost the phrase, a machine-bound bypass is available through us as a last resort. If neither works, the vault cannot be recovered.

I need to move Nexus to a new PC.

Keys are tied to one machine and checked offline. Contact us and we will help with a new key.

Where are the logs for a crash?

See the troubleshooting guide for where they live. They stay on your PC unless you choose to send one.
